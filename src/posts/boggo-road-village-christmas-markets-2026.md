New for 2026, The Market Folk are running two Christmas markets at Boggo Road Village in Dutton Park, a fortnight apart. Same venue, same organiser, same free entry, but they are quite different outings: one is a Sunday morning, the other a Friday evening after work.

If you can only make one, this side-by-side should help you choose. Everything below comes from The Market Folk's [Boggo Road Village Christmas Markets page](https://themarketfolk.com.au/pages/boggo-road-village-christmas-markets-2026) and their [2026 Christmas markets overview](https://themarketfolk.com.au/blogs/blog/christmas-markets-brisbane-2026), both checked on 18 November 2026.

> **Check the organiser's page before you go.** Outdoor markets in a Brisbane summer can change at short notice because of heat or storms, and stallholder line-ups are still to be published.

## Choose the Sunday morning (29 November) if…

**You want first pick for the season.** At the end of November, most people haven't started their Christmas shopping in earnest, and makers often bring their fullest range early in the season. If you are hunting for one-off ceramics or jewellery, earlier is generally better. The Market Folk's own tip is to arrive early for the best pick of one-off and limited pieces.

**You're buying anything that needs posting.** Gifts bought on 29 November leave plenty of room to get to interstate or overseas family. By 11 December you are getting closer to the postage cut-offs, so check Australia Post's dates if that matters.

**You'd like a booked pet portrait.** The Sunday market has Christmas pet photo sessions with Suzanne Joy Photography in front of a Christmas backdrop, and the organiser says you book a session on the photographer's website. If you want a particular time, booking is the safer option than turning up.

**Heat bothers you less in the morning.** A 9am start means the first hour or so is usually the most comfortable part of a summer day. By late morning, the sun is high, so bring a hat and water.

## Choose the Friday twilight (11 December) if…

**You work during the week and Sundays are taken.** A 4pm-to-8pm window means you can come straight from work or after an early dinner.

**You'd like a Santa photo with your dog.** At the twilight market, DK Imagery is taking Christmas pet photos with Santa. Session details will be shared on The Market Folk's Instagram closer to the day.

**You're finishing your list, not starting it.** By mid-December, many people have a short list of the last few people to buy for. A focused evening lap suits that well.

**You'd rather avoid the midday sun.** The evening is often cooler than late morning, though a Brisbane December afternoon can still be warm and sticky at 4pm, and storms are common at that time of year. Look at the Bureau of Meteorology forecast and radar before you leave.

## What's the same at both

- **Free entry, no booking.** Just turn up.
- **Dogs on a lead are welcome.** Bring water for them, and keep them clear of tables with breakable things.
- **Handpicked stallholders.** The Market Folk say every stallholder is handpicked, with a focus on original, locally designed products.

## Getting there

Boggo Road Village is in Dutton Park, close to the heritage-listed former Boggo Road Gaol. The organiser gives the address as 29 Boggo Road. They don't publish parking or transport advice on the event page, so plan your trip with the Translink journey planner or a map app. Public transport options in the area include the Boggo Road busway station and Park Road train station, but check the planner for your route and that day's timetable, especially on a Sunday morning.

## A quick plan for each date

**Sunday 29 November**

1. Check the organiser's page and Instagram the night before for the stallholder line-up and any weather updates.
2. If you want a pet portrait, book your Suzanne Joy Photography session ahead.
3. Arrive close to 9am for the best pick, with a hat, water and a sturdy reusable bag.
4. Write down maker names for anything you want to order later, especially personalised pieces that need lead time.

**Friday 11 December**

1. Check the organiser's page on the day, as storms can roll in on December afternoons.
2. Bring your list of the last few people still to buy for.
3. Arrive when you can, and keep heat-sensitive buys like chocolate and candles out of the hot car.
4. If your dog is coming for the Santa photo, check The Market Folk's Instagram for session details beforehand.
