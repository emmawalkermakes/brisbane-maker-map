The BrisStyle Twilight Market is one of the easiest handmade markets in Brisbane to get to and one of the nicest to wander on a warm evening. It sets up in King George Square in front of City Hall, and the November edition lands right as people start thinking seriously about Christmas gifts.

Here's what you need to know about the **Friday 13 November 2026** market, based on the organiser's information as of 1 November 2026.

> **Check the organiser's page before you go.** Evening storms are common in Brisbane in November, and any event can be changed or called off. The [BrisStyle Twilight Market page](https://www.brisstyle.com.au/brisstyle-twilight-market/) and BrisStyle's Facebook events are the places to confirm.

## What's on sale

BrisStyle describes the twilight market as selling **100% locally handmade** goods from Brisbane-based designers and artists. Their own examples include natural skincare, ceramics and wall art, and you can expect a wide mix beyond that, since stallholders vary from market to market.

What's useful about a market with a handmade focus is that you can usually talk to the person who made the thing. That matters if you're buying gifts:

- **Ask about care.** How should a ceramic mug be washed? Will a print fade in a sunny room? Makers know.
- **Ask about personalisation.** Some makers take custom orders, such as adding a name or changing a colour. Ask what their December lead times look like before you get your hopes up.
- **Ask about postage.** If you're buying for someone interstate, some stallholders will post directly. Others sell online too, so you can order later.

## Food and atmosphere

The organiser mentions food trucks and live music, set against the lit-up facade of City Hall. It's a good option for an after-work catch-up: grab dinner from a truck, do a lap of the stalls, and be home at a reasonable hour.

## Getting there

King George Square is in the middle of the CBD, which makes public transport the easy choice:

- **Buses:** King George Square has its own busway station underneath the square, and many city routes stop nearby.
- **Trains:** Central Station is a short walk up Ann Street, and Roma Street is also walkable.
- **Driving:** There's a car park under the square and others nearby, but city parking is expensive and fills up. If you must drive, check rates before you go.
- **Cycling:** There are bike racks around the CBD; bring a lock.

Use the Translink journey planner on the day to check services.

## Tips for a November evening market

**Arrive early for the best pick.** The market starts at 4pm. One-off pieces and small batches go first, so if you're on a mission for a specific gift, come early. If you're there for the atmosphere, later is cooler and livelier.

**Expect heat, then maybe a storm.** November afternoons in Brisbane can be hot and sticky, and storms often roll in late in the day. A small umbrella doubles as sun and rain cover. If a storm looks likely, check BrisStyle's social channels before you leave work.

**Bring a reusable bag with a flat base.** Ceramics, candles and framed prints travel better upright.

**Mix your payment options.** Most stallholders take cards, but busy squares can have patchy mobile reception. A little cash is a sensible backup.

**Make a short list.** It's easy to impulse-buy at a handmade market. Jot down who you're shopping for and a rough budget for each person before you go.

**Be kind to makers' time.** If a stall is busy, wait for a gap before asking detailed custom-order questions. Taking a business card and emailing later is often easier for everyone.

## Shopping for Christmas gifts at the market

A few gift categories that suit a handmade market well:

- **Small ceramics:** ring dishes, bud vases and espresso cups are easy to wrap and post.
- **Prints and cards:** flat, light and easy to send interstate.
- **Skincare and candles:** check ingredients for anyone with sensitivities, and keep candles out of a hot car on the way home.
- **Kids' items:** handmade toys and clothing. Ask about safety standards and age suitability for anything aimed at babies and toddlers.

If you're sending gifts interstate, remember that Australia Post's 2026 Christmas metro Parcel Post cut-offs from Queensland are mostly Monday 21 December, with earlier dates for WA, Tasmania and the NT. The [official Australia Post page](https://auspost.com.au/personal/sending/christmas-sending-dates) has the full table.
