Brisbane Maker Map is an independent hobby blog about the handmade side of Brisbane: markets where local makers sell their work, workshops where you can learn a craft for an afternoon, and the practical stuff (parking, heat, timing) that makes a market morning go well.

It's written by a Brisbane local who'd rather spend a Saturday wandering stalls than a shopping centre, and who got tired of piecing together dates from five different Instagram accounts.

## How dates are checked

- Every dated event on this site comes from the **organiser's own website** (or an official listing such as Visit Brisbane) and is linked so you can check it yourself.
- Each post and the [market calendar](/markets/) say when the details were last checked.
- Markets get cancelled for storms, venues change, and times shift in summer. **Always check the organiser's page before you go.**
- If an event can't be confirmed from an official source, it isn't listed.

## What you won't find here

No paid listings, no sponsored "top 10s", and no affiliation with any market organiser.

## Disclosure

This is a hobby blog. I make things myself, and if I ever have a stall at a market I write about, or mention something I've made, I'll say so clearly in that post.
